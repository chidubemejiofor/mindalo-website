What is true about dementia?

Many people have heard things about dementia that are not true. These ideas can stop families from getting help.

  • People say“Forgetting things is just part of getting old.”

    The factsSome forgetfulness is common as we age. Dementia is different. It is caused by disease in the brain, it gets worse over time, and it makes everyday life hard.

  • People say“Dementia is caused by a curse or witchcraft.”

    The factsDementia is a medical condition of the brain. It is nobody’s fault — not the person’s, and not the family’s.

  • People say“Nothing can be done.”

    The factsThere is no cure yet. But an early check, good care and support can help people live well for many years.

  • People say“People with dementia do not understand anything.”

    The factsPeople with dementia still have feelings, memories and things they enjoy. Talk with them, include them, and treat them with respect.

Dementia is growing fast

  • 57 millionpeople were living with dementia around the world in 2021
  • Over 60%of them live in low- and middle-income countries
  • Nearly 10 millionnew cases every year

Source: World Health Organization, dementia fact sheet.

Africa’s population is ageing fast, and dementia services have not kept up. Mindalo is here to help change that — one family, one community, one conversation at a time.
