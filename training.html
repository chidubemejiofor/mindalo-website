Dementia training and education

Mindalo offers practical dementia training across Africa. Our sessions help health workers, carers and communities recognise dementia earlier and care for people with dignity. Good training matters because many people meet dementia for the first time with no preparation. Every session is planned to be useful the very next day.

Who it is for

  • Community health workers
  • Nurses, doctors and other clinical staff
  • Care home and home care staff
  • Faith and community leaders
  • Families and carers

What we cover

  • What dementia is, and the main types
  • Spotting early signs, and when to refer
  • Talking with people who have dementia
  • Care that puts the person first and protects their dignity
  • Caring for people with dementia and other conditions, such as diabetes or high blood pressure
  • Supporting families, and tackling stigma and myths

Ways we can work with you

We offer training in three formats. Each one can run in person or online. This lets us fit the training to your setting, your time and your budget. Choose the format that suits your group best.

  • Community talks

    Short, friendly sessions for community groups, places of worship and schools.

  • Workshops for staff

    Half-day or full-day training for health and care teams.

  • Made for your organisation

    Sessions planned around your staff, your setting and your needs.

How to ask for training, step by step

  1. 1

    Email us

    Tell us who the training is for, how many people, and where you are.

  2. 2

    We plan it with you

    We talk about what your team needs and agree the format, date and cost.

  3. 3

    We run the training

    We deliver the session and share resources your team can keep using.
