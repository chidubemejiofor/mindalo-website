Mayelana nathi

Mayelana ne-Mindalo

I-Mindalo ikhona ukuze ivale igebe phakathi kokuxilongwa kwesifo sokukhohlwa (dementia), ukunakekelwa nokuqondwa kwaso e-Afrika yonke. Sisebenza kulo lonke uhambo, kusukela ezimpawini zokuqala kuze kube wukuphila kahle nesifo sokukhohlwa. Lokhu kubalulekile ngoba imindeni eminingi kakhulu ibhekana nalolo hambo inosizo oluncane. Sikholwa wukuthi ukunakekelwa okuhle kwesifo sokukhohlwa kuqala ngesithunzi.

Kungani sikhona

Isifo sokukhohlwa sikhuphuka kakhulu e-Afrika yonke. Nokho ukuxilongwa kuvame ukufika sekwephuzile, abanakekeli bavame ukushiywa bengenakwesekwa, futhi abantu abaningi basasiqonda kabi lesi simo. Abantu base-Afrika baguga ngokushesha, kodwa ukunakekelwa kwesifo sokukhohlwa akukahambisani nalokho. I-Mindalo ikhona ukuze isize ukushintsha lokho — umndeni owodwa, umphakathi owodwa, ingxoxo eyodwa ngesikhathi.

Esikwenzayo

Umsebenzi wethu ulandela uhambo lwesifo sokukhohlwa ngezigaba ezintathu. Isigaba ngasinye sibhekana nesidingo esihlukile, kusukela ekutholeni izimpendulo, ekutholeni ukwesekwa, kuya ekwakheni ukuqonda. Esikukholelwayo

  • Isithunzi siza kuqala — somuntu ophila nesifo sokukhohlwa, kanye nesabantu abamnakekelayo.
  • Amagama acacile ayasiza — sichaza izinto ngendlela elula, ngaphandle kwamagama anzima obuchwepheshe.
  • Imiphakathi yazi kangcono — sisebenza nemindeni, abasebenzi bezempilo nabaholi bendawo.
  • Ulwazi kufanele luthembeke — umsebenzi wethu usekelwe ocwaningweni nasezindleleni ezinhle zokusebenza.

Sebenza nathi

Asikwazi ukuvala leli gebe sisodwa. Ozakwethu basisiza ukuthi sifinyelele emindenini eminingi futhi siqeqeshe abasebenzi bezempilo abaningi. Uzakwethu ngamunye uletha amakhono, ukufinyelela noma izinsiza esingenazo thina sodwa. Singathanda ukuzwa kulaba abalandelayo:

  • izinsiza zezempilo, imitholampilo nezibhedlela
  • amanyuvesi nabacwaningi
  • izinhlangano zomphakathi nezenkolo
  • abaxhasi nabanikeli
  • amavolontiya anesikhathi noma amakhono okwabelana ngawo
