Uqeqesho nemfundo ngesifo sokukhohlwa (dementia)

I-Mindalo inikeza uqeqesho olusebenzayo ngesifo sokukhohlwa e-Afrika yonke. Izifundo zethu zisiza abasebenzi bezempilo, abanakekeli nemiphakathi ukuthi babone isifo sokukhohlwa kusenesikhathi futhi banakekele abantu ngesithunzi. Uqeqesho oluhle lubalulekile ngoba abantu abaningi bahlangana nesifo sokukhohlwa okokuqala bengazilungiselele. Isifundo ngasinye sihlelwe ukuthi sibe wusizo kanye ngosuku olulandelayo.

Lwenzelwe obani

  • Abasebenzi bezempilo basemphakathini
  • Onesi, odokotela nabanye abasebenzi bezokwelapha
  • Abasebenzi bamakhaya okunakekela nabanakekela abantu emakhaya
  • Abaholi bezenkolo nabomphakathi
  • Imindeni nabanakekeli

Esikufundisayo

  • Siyini isifo sokukhohlwa, nezinhlobo zaso eziyinhloko
  • Ukubona izimpawu zokuqala, nokuthi umuntu kufanele adluliselwe nini
  • Ukukhuluma nabantu abanesifo sokukhohlwa
  • Ukunakekela okubeka umuntu kuqala futhi kuvikele isithunzi sakhe
  • Ukunakekela abantu abanesifo sokukhohlwa nezinye izimo, ezifana nesifo sikashukela noma umfutho wegazi ophezulu
  • Ukweseka imindeni, nokulwa nokucwaswa kanye nezinkolelo ezingelona iqiniso

Izindlela esingasebenza ngazo nawe

Sinikeza uqeqesho ngezindlela ezintathu. Ngayinye ingenziwa ubuso nobuso noma ku-inthanethi. Lokhu kusivumela ukuthi silungise uqeqesho lufanele indawo yakho, isikhathi sakho nesabelomali sakho. Khetha indlela efanela iqembu lakho kakhulu.

  • Izinkulumo zomphakathi

    Izifundo ezimfushane nezinobungane zamaqembu omphakathi, izindawo zokukhonza nezikole.

  • Imihlangano yokuqeqesha abasebenzi

    Uqeqesho lwengxenye yosuku noma losuku lonke lwamathimba ezempilo nawokunakekela.

  • Okwenzelwe inhlangano yakho

    Izifundo ezihlelwe ngokwabasebenzi bakho, indawo yakho nezidingo zakho.

Indlela yokucela uqeqesho, isinyathelo ngesinyathelo

  1. 1

    Sithumelele i-imeyili

    Sitshele ukuthi uqeqesho lwenzelwa obani, bangaki abantu, nokuthi nikuphi.

  2. 2

    Siluhlela kanye nawe

    Sikhuluma ngalokho okudingwa yithimba lakho bese sivumelana ngendlela, usuku nezindleko.

  3. 3

    Siqhuba uqeqesho

    Senza isifundo futhi sabelane ngezinsiza ithimba lakho elingaqhubeka lizisebenzisa.
