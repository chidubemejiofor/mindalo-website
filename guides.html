Guides for carers

These guides are for anyone caring for a person with dementia. They are short, use plain words, and are listed in the order most families need them. Each one starts with the main points, so you can find what matters quickly. Start with the first guide, or go straight to the one you need.

Need something we have not covered?

We want these guides to answer your real questions. If something is missing, tell us what would help. We use your ideas to decide which guides to write next. Email us at hello@mindalo.org.

Every page has tools to make reading easier. You can make the words bigger, listen to the page, or print it. The buttons are in the strip just below the menu. Use whichever way of reading suits you best.
