About us

About Mindalo

Mindalo exists to close the gap between dementia diagnosis, care and understanding across Africa.

Why we exist

Dementia is rising sharply across Africa. But diagnosis often comes late, carers are often left without support, and many people still do not understand the condition. Too many families face a hard journey largely alone.

Africa’s population is ageing fast, and dementia care has not kept pace. Mindalo is here to help change that — one family, one community, one conversation at a time.

What we do

Our work follows the whole dementia journey.

  1. Diagnosis

    Earlier recognition

    Supporting earlier recognition of dementia and better access to assessment.

  2. Support

    Families and carers

    Resources and community for the people who give care every day.

  3. Education

    Public understanding

    Awareness and training that reduce stigma and misinformation.

What we believe

  • Dignity comes first — for the person living with dementia, and for the people caring for them.
  • Clear words help — we explain things simply, without jargon.
  • Communities know best — we work with families, health workers and local leaders.
  • Information must be sound — our work is based on research and good practice.
